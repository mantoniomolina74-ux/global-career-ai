\## Purpose



The Dashboard Experience represents the user-facing intelligence layer of Global Career AI.



Its responsibility is to transform the outputs generated by internal intelligence engines into a coherent, explainable and actionable professional experience.



The Dashboard does not calculate intelligence.

It consumes and presents intelligence.



\---



\# 1. Architectural Vision



The Dashboard is the Intelligence Experience Hub of Global Career AI.



It connects:



\- Intelligence Engines

\- Aggregation Layer

\- Data Contracts

\- User Interfaces



The Dashboard acts as the bridge between system intelligence and professional decision making.



\---



\# 2. Core Principle



\## Intelligence Producers vs Intelligence Consumers



Global Career AI follows a strict separation:



\### Intelligence Producers



Internal engines responsible for generating insights:



\- ATS Engine

\- Matching Engine

\- Decision Engine

\- Competency Intelligence

\- Knowledge Intelligence

\- Learning Intelligence

\- Evidence Layer

\- Recommendation Engine





\### Intelligence Consumer



The Dashboard consumes these results through stable contracts.



The UI never communicates directly with internal engines.

\# 4. Architectural Goals



The Dashboard must provide:



\- Explainable intelligence

\- Clear professional insights

\- Modular evolution

\- Future AI capability integration

\- Multi-platform support



\---



\# 5. Non Goals



The Dashboard will not:



\- Execute business logic

\- Replace intelligence engines

\- Calculate scores

\- Modify engine behavior



\---



\# 6. Evolution Strategy



The Dashboard architecture must allow future integration of:



\- Interview Intelligence

\- Salary Intelligence

\- Market Intelligence

\- Career Prediction Models

\- AI Career Agents



without redesigning the core architecture.



\---



\# 7. Dashboard Domain Architecture



The Dashboard is organized into independent intelligence domains.



Each domain represents a specific professional perspective and consumes intelligence through the Dashboard Aggregation Layer.
